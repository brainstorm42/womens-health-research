# Status taxonomy

Use one primary status for each public research item. Do not combine a planned question with a completed result.

## Proposal

A question, hypothesis, project idea, or planned test. It may be useful and well motivated, but it is not evidence that the proposed pattern is true.

**Minimum record:** question, intended data/source, falsifiable test, main limitation, and next review step.

## Reviewed static finding

A source-level statement checked against a named paper, dataset record, protocol, or official page at a stated access level. It is not automatically a new experiment, an independent biological validation, or a clinical conclusion.

**Minimum record:** exact source, locator, access level, wording boundary, reviewer type, and residual uncertainty.

## Provisional numerical result

A calculation or reproduction using public or synthetic data with enough provenance for another contributor to inspect. It remains provisional until independent checks of the data, code, transformations, and interpretation are complete.

**Minimum record:** data and code versions, computation steps, checks, uncertainty, exclusions, reproduction status, and interpretation limits.

## Access or rights pending

The relevant source, method, version, or reuse permission has not been verified. This label records an unresolved state; it does not say that the source or result is absent.

## Independent AI check

An AI-assisted comparison or consistency check. It can identify likely transcription, locator, or wording problems, but it is not human expert review and should never be presented as such.

## Human expert review

A qualified person has reviewed the stated material and scope. Record the review date, expertise relevant to the question, exact files or claims reviewed, and any remaining disagreement. Human review of a source statement does not imply clinical endorsement.

## Not clinical advice

This is a standing boundary for the repository. Public research notes must not diagnose, recommend treatment, establish safety or efficacy for an individual, or substitute for care from a qualified health professional.
