## Sources requiring follow-up

### Wernert et al. (1992) — access gap

**Citation:** Wernert N, Albrech M, Sesterhenn I, Goebbels R, Bonkhoff H, Seitz G, Inniger R, Remberger K. “The 'female prostate': location, morphology, immunohistochemical characteristics and significance.” *European Urology*. 1992;22(1):64–69. DOI: [`10.1159/000474724`](https://doi.org/10.1159/000474724). PMID: [`1385145`](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1385145/). Publisher record: [Karger](https://karger.com/eur/article/22/1/64/131063/The-Female-Prostate-Location-Morphology).

**Needed for:** anatomical sampling and landmarks; separate PSA/PAP denominators and staining controls; duct-to-urethra continuity; original histology figures and captions; and a careful check of which functional or androgenic statements are measurements versus interpretation.

**Lawful-access record:** the publisher full-text and PDF controls were checked and redirected to the abstract. PubMed, DOI/publisher, exact-title/PDF, institutional-repository, and author-site searches were checked. No matching institutional or author open copy was verified. No login, purchase, contact, or access-barrier bypass was used.

**Status:** Full text was not verified in the bounded search. **Access gap; library-mediated lawful access and reuse review pending.**

### Huffman (1948) — parked

**Citation:** Huffman JW. “The detailed anatomy of the paraurethral ducts in the adult human female.” *American Journal of Obstetrics and Gynecology*. 1948;55(1):86–101. DOI: [`10.1016/0002-9378(48)90157-4`](https://doi.org/10.1016/0002-9378%2848%2990157-4). PMID: [`18918954`](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18918954/). Publisher record: [ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0002937848901574).

**Needed for:** serial-section and wax-reconstruction methods; duct openings, extent, and orientation relative to explicit landmarks; original reconstruction figures/captions; and the evidential basis of the attributed secretory activity.

**Lawful-access record:** the publisher indexed abstract was visible but offered institutional sign-in; no matching complete open copy was verified. PubMed, exact-title/PDF, institutional-repository, and author-site searches were included in the bounded search. A similarly titled 1948 review item and an isolated diagram were not substituted for this primary article or treated as rights clearance. No login, purchase, contact, or access-barrier bypass was used.

**Status:** **Parked** for a later library-access pass. The metadata and abstract-level lead are retained; full text, methods, figures, and reuse rights remain unresolved.

## Search boundary

The retained search boundary was publisher/DOI, PubMed/PMC, exact-title/PDF, institutional-repository, and author-site lookup. No accounts, purchases, external contact, paywall bypass, or redistribution occurred. A readable abstract or a public search result is not the same as a cleared full text or image-reuse license.

## What a future update should add

For each source, add only: the lawful route used, version/date, exact full-text coverage, method/figure locators, rights status, and an independent-review outcome. Keep source PDFs, screenshots, patient or donor images, and library downloads outside this public repository unless rights are separately verified.
